Selling crypto for cash sounds simple until you actually try it. The first wall you hit is the choice: do you want speed, the best rate, or a payout method that actually works in your country? This guide walks through the realistic ways to convert crypto into fiat — with a focus on how OKX handles each route, where the fees actually sit, and which method fits which situation. The goal is to help you pick the right path the first time instead of learning the differences by trial and error.

## What "selling crypto for cash" really means

When people say "sell crypto for cash," they usually mean one of three things:

- Converting crypto into a fiat balance on an exchange, then withdrawing it to a bank account, card, or e-wallet
- Selling crypto directly to another person through a peer-to-peer (P2P) marketplace and receiving local currency via bank transfer, mobile money, or cash app
- Using an off-ramp service (Visa/Mastercard, PayPal, SEPA, ACH) that pays out to a linked external account

All three end with fiat in your hands, but the speed, cost, and supported regions differ a lot. The method that's "best" depends less on the coin you're selling and more on where you live, how much you're selling, and how fast you need the money.

## The three ways to sell crypto on OKX

OKX groups its sell options into three core methods. Each one targets a different kind of user, and the differences are concrete enough that picking the wrong one will cost you either time or money.

### Express Sell: the fast, fixed-rate route

Express Sell is OKX's one-click crypto-to-fiat conversion. You pick the coin, the fiat currency, and the amount, see a quote with the rate and fee spelled out, and confirm. The fiat lands in your linked bank account, card, or e-wallet — usually within minutes for supported methods.

This is the path most beginners should take. The rate is fixed at the moment you confirm, so you don't watch the price slip while the order fills. The trade-off is that you don't get to shop around for a better price; you accept OKX's quoted rate, which includes a small spread.

Express Sell supports major coins (BTC, ETH, USDT, USDC and others) and over 30 fiat currencies including USD, EUR, GBP, INR, IDR, NGN and BRL. It's best for one-off cash-outs, typically under $20,000, where speed matters more than squeezing out the last fraction of a percent.

### P2P Trading: zero-fee, maximum flexibility

OKX P2P is a peer-to-peer marketplace where you sell directly to other verified users. OKX doesn't charge a trading fee on P2P — the platform explicitly states "zero fee when you trade" — and supports over 100 local payment methods.

The mechanism is escrow-based: when you accept a buyer's order, OKX locks your crypto in escrow. The buyer sends payment through the method you agreed on (local bank, PayPal, mobile money, cash deposit, etc.). Once you confirm the payment has actually arrived in your account, you release the crypto. If something goes wrong, you can open a dispute through the in-app chat and OKX support steps in.

P2P makes sense when:

- Your local currency or payment method isn't supported by Express Sell
- You want to avoid exchange spreads and accept a price set by another user
- You're comfortable verifying payments manually and managing the trade flow

The catch is that external payment providers may still charge transfer fees, and trade speed depends on how fast the buyer pays — typically 10 to 30 minutes, but it can drag out.

### Spot Sell: best rate, more steps

Spot Sell means placing a market or limit order on OKX's spot market, usually against a stablecoin (USDT, USDC) or a fiat pair (BTC/EUR, ETH/USD). You get the best execution price and the lowest fees, but you then have to move the proceeds to your Funding account and withdraw through a separate fiat withdrawal step.

This is the route for active traders and anyone selling larger amounts where a few basis points matter. Spot fees for regular users start at 0.10% taker on the standard schedule, and limit (maker) orders can be cheaper. VIP tiers drop those rates further — down to 0.0150% taker at VIP 9.

## Choosing the right method for your situation

### First-time seller, small amount, want it fast

Use **Express Sell**. The fixed rate means no surprises, the payout lands in minutes for supported bank/card methods, and you don't have to negotiate with another user. Keep the amount under $20,000 to stay within typical Express limits.

### Selling in a local currency Express doesn't support

Use **P2P Trading**. Filter for your local payment method, pick a buyer with a high completion rate and lots of completed trades, and follow the escrow flow. The zero platform fee means more of the value stays with you; the trade-off is that you have to manually verify each payment.

### Selling a large amount where every basis point matters

Use **Spot Sell** with a limit (maker) order to get the maker fee, then withdraw via the cheapest fiat route for your region. If you're a VIP user, the maker fee drops further and turns negative at VIP 7+. For amounts above $50,000, OKX also offers an OTC desk with tailored pricing — contact support to access it.

### Need cash the same day in the US or EU

Express Sell to ACH (US) or SEPA (EU) is the fastest practical route. Both can credit same day, sometimes within minutes, with low fees. SWIFT is the fallback for global transfers but expect 1–3 business days.

### Cashing out in a region with limited banking

P2P is usually the only realistic option. Look for verified merchants (badged users) and payment methods that work locally — mobile money, cash deposit, regional e-wallets. The escrow protects you, but you still need to confirm payment before releasing.

## Common pitfalls and how to avoid them

**Releasing P2P crypto before payment lands.** This is the single most common way people lose money on P2P. Always confirm the payment is in your account through your own bank or payment app, not via the buyer's screenshot. If you're unsure, open a dispute.

**Mixing up sell fee and withdrawal fee.** The 0.10% taker fee on a spot sell is not the same as the $15 SWIFT withdrawal fee. They stack. Calculate both before deciding which route is cheapest for your amount.

**Hitting a withdrawal limit you didn't know about.** Withdrawal limits are rolling and reset based on when each transaction hits, not on a calendar day. Check your remaining limit on the withdrawal screen before planning a large cash-out.

**Forgetting KYC.** All fiat withdrawals on OKX require identity verification. If you try to sell before completing KYC, you'll get stopped at the withdrawal step. Do it upfront — it usually takes minutes.

**Picking Express when Spot would save you money.** Express is convenient, but the spread is built into the rate. On a $50,000 sale, the difference between Express and a limit order on spot can be meaningful. Run both quotes if the amount is large.
